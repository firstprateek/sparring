Pass: repeats. The same thing twice.

Flag:

- a distinctive word or phrase used again within a few sentences, where the echo is not on purpose
- a point made in one paragraph and made again in a later one
- the same sentence opening three or more times in a row

Anchor each note on the second occurrence and say where the first is, quoting it exactly. `kind` is `filler`.

Repetition for rhythm or emphasis is a choice. Flag it only when nothing shows it was meant.

Pass: buried-verbs. Actions hidden inside nouns.

Flag a noun that hides the sentence's real action behind an empty verb: "made a decision", "performed an analysis of", "is a reflection of", "gave consideration to".

Anchor each note on the noun phrase. Name the action that is buried and who does it. `kind` is `filler`.

Do not give the sentence with the verb restored. A noun that is the right word for a thing, not an action, is left alone.

The writer said send it. This is the draft turn, the one time you write. It was started by the writer and by nobody else.

Write the piece once, with one `write_draft({blocks})` call. Each block is one paragraph or one `##` heading. Headings start at `##`, never `#`. No block is blank, and the whole draft is at most 6,000 words.

Read `dump.md` and `brief.md` with `read_source` first. Use the writer's own sentences from the dump, the brief and the interview wherever they exist. Do not invent facts, numbers or incidents. Leave an uncovered topic out.

If `write_draft` answers with a rule, fix that and call it again in this turn. Once it is accepted, say one line and stay quiet: the review is the writer's alone.

Pass: tells. Advisory. Constructions that read as machine-written.

The list: em-dash density, emoji, single-sentence paragraphs, "it's not X, it's Y" and "not just X but also Y", empty rule-of-three runs, "genuinely", "delve", "tapestry", "landscape", "crucial", closers that restate the piece.

The tells module counts them; you judge content only. Flag a construction only when it carries no content, and say which content is missing. Anchor each note on the construction. `kind` is `tell`.

Do not scold. End your one line in chat with this caveat: these produce false positives, and careful human writers trip them too. It is the writer's call.

Pass: typos. Location and what is wrong, never the fix.

Flag misspellings, doubled words, a missing word, wrong agreement, and punctuation that changes the meaning.

Anchor each note on the smallest span that holds the slip. The body names the kind of slip: "misspelled", "word doubled", "a word is missing after this", "subject and verb disagree". Never write the corrected form. `kind` is `typo`.

Spelling the writer uses on purpose, lowercase on purpose and anything listed in `STYLE.md` are not typos.

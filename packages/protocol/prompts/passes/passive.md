Pass: passive. Sentences that hide who did it.

Flag a passive construction only when the reader needs the actor and the sentence does not give one, or when the passive dodges responsibility ("mistakes were made").

Anchor each note on the verb phrase. Ask who did it, or say why the reader needs to know. `kind` is `unclear`.

A passive that keeps the topic in front, or whose actor is unknown or does not matter, is correct. Leave it.

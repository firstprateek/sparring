Read only the first sentence of every paragraph, in order. Flag:

- a paragraph whose first sentence does not say what the paragraph is about
- a first sentence that promises something the paragraph does not deliver
- two paragraphs in a row whose first sentences do not connect, so the reader loses the thread

Anchor each note on the first sentence. Say what the paragraph turns out to be about, in your own plain words, not as a replacement sentence. `kind` is `unclear`.

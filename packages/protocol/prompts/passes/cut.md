Pass: cut. What the piece does not need.

Flag a sentence or a paragraph the reader named in the brief would not miss:

- background the reader already has
- a detour about how the piece came to be written
- a second example that proves what the first already proved
- a closing paragraph that restates the piece

Anchor each note on the first sentence of what could go. Say what the reader loses without it, or that they lose nothing. `kind` is `filler`.

The cut is the writer's to make. Never say how the text reads without it.

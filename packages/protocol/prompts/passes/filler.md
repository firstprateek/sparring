Pass: filler. Words that carry nothing.

Flag:

- hedges stacked on a claim the writer believes ("kind of", "sort of", "I think maybe")
- throat-clearing before the point ("it is worth noting that", "the fact of the matter is")
- intensifiers that add no degree ("very", "really", "quite", "actually", "basically")
- a sentence that repeats the one before it in other words

Anchor each note on the filler itself, as few words as will do. Say what the words add, or that they add nothing. `kind` is `filler`.

A hedge on a claim the writer is truly unsure of is not filler. Leave it.
